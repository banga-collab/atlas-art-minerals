The workshop

Raw blocks in. Finished pieces out.

A family business since 1981. Many of our artisans have carved with us for decades. Cutting, finishing, packing and export happen under one roof, so you deal with the people who make your order.

Inside the workshop

How trade ordering works

No account needed. Prices, minimums and lead times depend on the piece, the quantity and the destination, so we quote each order.

  1. Build your list

    Add pieces with rough quantities, plus sizes or colours if you know them.

  2. Send it

    By WhatsApp or email in one tap, with your company and country.

  3. Get your quote

    Trade prices, minimum per piece, lead time and a shipping estimate.

  4. We carve & ship

    Cut, finished and packed in Agadir, shipped worldwide.
