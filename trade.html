Questions buyers ask

Before you order.

What's your minimum order?

It depends on the piece. Small holders and larger lamps have different minimums. Send your list and we'll confirm the minimum for each item in your quote.

Why can't I see prices online?

Trade prices depend on quantity, size, finish and destination. We'd rather send you an exact quote than a list price that doesn't fit your order.

Can you customise a piece?

Yes, often. Many pieces can change in size, finish or packaging, and we develop new shapes with brands. Send a sketch or photo and we'll tell you what's possible.

Do you ship to my country?

We ship worldwide. Costs and delivery times depend on your destination and the size of the order, so we include a shipping estimate in every quote.

Will every piece look like the photo?

Close, but not identical. Selenite and onyx are natural stones, so grain, tone and banding vary from piece to piece. That's part of their character.

How should selenite be cared for?

Keep it dry and dust it with a soft cloth. Selenite is soft and doesn't like water, so it isn't for outdoor use or contact with food.

Can I visit before ordering?

Yes. Wholesale buyers are welcome at our workshop and showroom in Agadir by appointment. Get in touch to arrange a date.
