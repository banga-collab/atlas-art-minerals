The workshop · Agadir

A family trade in stone, since 1981.

Atlas Art Minerals is run by the Habbad family. We started with Moroccan stone and minerals, and selenite became our specialty. Today more than a hundred people carve, finish, pack and ship from Agadir.

The craft

Skills we brought to Morocco, then made our own.

Several of the techniques we use were new to the country when we started. We trained our artisans in them ourselves, and many have now been with us for decades. That continuity is why a reorder matches the first order.

Selenite is soft, so most of the work is by hand: splitting the raw block, shaping, sanding and finishing. Pieces are left raw, polished smooth, or edged with gold leaf.
